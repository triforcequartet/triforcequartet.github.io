Your Wedding
Music That's
Uniquely You (nerd)

Your wedding music should sound like the two of you, not a standard playlist. We work with you to choose the pieces for each part of the day, from the ceremony to the reception, so the music becomes part of what you and your guests remember.

We're a professional, classically trained string quartet, and we play everything from traditional wedding repertoire to our specialty, video game music. We've performed over 100 weddings, 50 conventions, and countless other shows across the country, and we bring the same care to a ceremony as we do to a big stage performance.

Elegant. Emotional. Unforgettable.

Wedding planning involves a lot of decisions, and keeping everyone aligned isn’t always easy. We want the music to be one of the parts that feels straightforward. Because we perform across a range of genres, including classical, pop, rock, broadway, and (of course) video game music, you can shape a set that fits you as a couple instead of picking from a short standard list.

Not sure what to choose? We have a ‘wedding worksheet’ to help you organize your thoughts, and we’ll talk through the tougher calls with you.

Quick Snapshot: What We Can Play

  • Ceremony Music

    Processional, recessional, any mid-ceremony interludes

  • Cocktail Hour & Dinner

    Background music that your guests love

  • First Dances & Special Moments

    Romantic game themes, classical pieces, or anything else you desire

  • Reception Set

    Upbeat selections to delight guests and gamers alike

What Sets Us Apart

  • Versatile Repertoire: Classical, Video Game Music, plus everything in between!

    We are best known for our video game arrangements, but every member is a classically trained professional musician, and we play the traditional wedding repertoire with the same care. We build the setlist around what you actually want, whether that is timeless, modern, a little nerdy, or a mix of all three.

  • Experience Brought to Your Wedding Day

    We have played a LOT of weddings, so we know the details that keep a day running smoothly. We coordinate directly with your planner, photographer, videographer, and venue staff, so the timing and logistics are handled and you are not managing the music on your wedding day.

  • Original Arrangements Only We Have

    Most of our catalog is arranged by us, in-house. That means you are not limited to a fixed set list: Also, if there is a specific song or theme that matters to the two of you and we do not already play it, we can arrange it for you. Want your processional to be a piece no other couple has walked down the aisle to? That is the kind of thing we love to make happen.

  • Professional and Travel-Ready

    Based in the Washington, D.C. area (Fairfax VA), we have played weddings of every size, indoors and out, and we will sort out travel, tech, and timing to match your plans. We even have ana PA system we can bring with us!
