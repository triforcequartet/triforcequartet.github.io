- 
    ### Real Video Game Fans, Professional Musicians

    We grew up on these games and soundtracks. Every member of Triforce Quartet is a classically trained string player and a longtime gamer, so our video game music arrangements come from people who know the source material from the inside. We are not a string quartet padding a set with a couple of game themes. This is the music we care about most, played with the discipline of classical training. Your attendees will hear that difference the moment we start.
- 
    ### Perfect for Any Convention Setting

    We slot into whatever your programming needs: a main stage concert, a panel performance, live background music for a VIP lounge, or playing at a formal ball. We have played fan conventions, anime expos, comic cons, gaming industry events, academic and orchestral showcases, and more! We know how to work with your run time, your stage, and the tech you have on site.
- 
    ### Washington DC Based & Tour-Ready

    Based in the Washington, D.C. area in Fairfax VA, we travel across the country bringing epic performances to fans everywhere. Triforce Quartet is ready to work with your team to create a performance experience tailored to your event. We can (classically!) rock out as a headliner for an evening show, jam with other artists during a special guest appearance, speak about our craft as a featured panelist, or provide background music for varying events on the convention schedule. Our versatility in repertoire and experience invites creativity when planning an event.
- 
    ### Customizable Setlists

    If you want to feature particular games, franchises, or themes, we will shape the setlist around them. Our library reaches back decades, from early chiptune-era classics to modern orchestral scores. Most of it we arranged ourselves, which is worth knowing: the group you book had a hand in every stage of what your audience hears, from choosing the pieces to the final performance. That level of involvement is rare among quartets for hire.
