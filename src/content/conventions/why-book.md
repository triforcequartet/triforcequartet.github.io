- 
    ### Real Video Game Fans, Professional Musicians

    We grew up on these games and soundtracks. Every member of Triforce Quartet is a classically trained string player and a longtime gamer, so our video game music arrangements come from people who know the source material from the inside. We are not a string quartet padding a set with a couple of game themes. This is the music we care about most. Your attendees will hear that difference the moment we start.
- 
    ### Perfect for Any Convention Setting

    What type of convention or event is this for? Not only do we play "main stage" concerts, we also can play a panel performance, live background music for a VIP lounge, or playing at a formal ball. SinceOur acoustic instruments give us the flexibility of playing with or without amplification, and in almost any location! Feel free to be creative! We know how to work with your run time, your stage, and the tech you have on site.
- 
    ### Washington DC Based & Travel-Ready

    Based in the Washington, D.C. area in Fairfax VA, we travel across the country bringing our performances to fans everywhere. Since we have acoustic instruments, it is easy for us to fly or drive to various locations anywhere! We have played gaming conventions, anime expos, comic cons, gaming industry events, and more all over the Wolrd!
    
- 
    ### Customizable Setlists

    If you want to feature particular games, franchises, or themes, we will shape the setlist around them. Our library is vast and varied, from early era classics to modern orchestral scores. Most of it we arranged ourselves, so you know the group you book had a hand in every stage of what your audience hears.
